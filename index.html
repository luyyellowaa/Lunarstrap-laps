Wiki

Answers to the questions that come up most. This mirrors the topics covered on the project wiki, condensed for quick reading.

What are FastFlags, and is it safe to touch them?+

FastFlags are Roblox's own feature-flag system — small switches the client checks to decide how parts of the engine behave, like rendering, networking, or UI. Roblox normally controls them remotely; the FastFlags page lets you override specific ones locally instead.

The presets (Exclusive Fullscreen, DPI scaling fixes, Framerate limit, and so on) wrap the relevant flags for you, so you don't need to know the raw flag names to use them. If you go into the manual editor, only add flags you actually understand — a long list of flags you copied from somewhere else is the most common cause of a client that won't start.

Roblox crashes, hangs, or won't open at all+

Before assuming it's Lunarstrap, temporarily launch Roblox without it — if the same crash happens, the cause is on Roblox's side, not the bootstrapper. If it only happens through Lunarstrap, that's worth reporting.

Common patterns and where to look first:

  • The process opens and immediately closes — usually background software (overlays, some antivirus, RGB/utility apps) conflicting with Roblox.
  • It hangs at "Connecting to Roblox…" — often a corrupted local cache; clearing the Roblox cache folder resolves most of these.
  • It opens, then crashes moments later, consistently — check for outdated GPU drivers first.
  • An "Unapproved third-party applications or extensions detected" dialog appears — Roblox's anti-tamper flagged something injected into the process; remove third-party overlays and mods and try again.
How do custom mods work?+

Mods let you replace individual game files — textures, sounds, fonts — without touching Roblox's own installation. Lunarstrap keeps your modded files separate and re-applies them automatically every time Roblox updates, so you don't lose them on the next patch.

Drop replacement files into the mods folder from the Mods page, matching the original file's path and name, and Lunarstrap layers them in at launch.

Switching between the stock Roblox launcher and Lunarstrap+

Once installed, Lunarstrap registers itself as the handler for Roblox's roblox-player:// launch links, so clicking Play on the website opens through Lunarstrap from then on. To go back to the stock launcher, reinstall the Roblox app from the official site — that re-registers Roblox's own handler.
